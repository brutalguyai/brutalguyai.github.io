The Ember & Oak team

A small roaster, a slow rhythm.

Ember & Oak started in a tiny workshop in Lisbon with one secondhand drum roaster and a stubborn idea: coffee should arrive fresh, not months later.

So we roast just one day a week. Every bean we sell was roasted for someone who ordered it. We buy from importers who work directly with farms, and we pay for quality.
