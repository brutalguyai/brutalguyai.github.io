    FAQ

    Questions? Answered.

    What happens if I miss a day?+

    No shame spirals here. If you have a Streak Freeze, it's used automatically. If not, your streak resets — but your longest streak and all your history stay safe, and we'll help you bounce back with a gentle "comeback" plan.

    Is the free plan really free?+

    Yes — forever. Track up to 5 habits with streaks and reminders at no cost, no credit card required. Upgrade only if you want unlimited habits and the advanced features.

    Does Streakly work on Android and web?+

    Streakly is available on iOS and Android, with a web dashboard and Apple Watch / Wear OS companions. Everything syncs instantly across your devices.

    Is my data private?+

    Absolutely. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, we never sell it, and you can export or delete everything with a single tap.

    Can I cancel anytime?+

    Of course. Cancel from your account settings in seconds. You'll keep Pro features until the end of your billing period and drop back to the free plan afterward.
