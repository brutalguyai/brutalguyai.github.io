    Questions about Streakly

    What happens if I miss a day?

    If you have a Streak Freeze, it's used automatically. If not, your streak resets, but your longest streak and all your history stay safe. Streakly then suggests a gentle comeback plan.

    Is the free plan really free?

    Yes, forever. Track up to 5 habits with streaks and reminders at no cost and with no credit card. Upgrade only if you want unlimited habits and the advanced features.

    Does Streakly work on Android and web?

    Streakly is available on iOS and Android, with a web dashboard and Apple Watch and Wear OS companions. Everything syncs instantly across your devices.

    Is my data private?

    Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, we never sell it, and you can export or delete everything with a single tap.

    Can I cancel anytime?

    Cancel from your account settings in seconds. You keep Pro features until the end of your billing period, then drop back to the free plan.
